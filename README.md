# RBC Momentum Career Connect

A prototype portal that gives RBC early talent a voice, and gives departments the insight to keep them.

## Problem Statement

Hear the voice of early talent participants and their experiences. Flag disengaged participants anonymously back to their department, and identify common issues within those departments to remediate, with the goal of increasing early talent retention.

Today, feedback is limited to formal midterm and mid-year reviews. RBC Momentum is one solution where all RBC interns can connect and give continuous feedback throughout their term, not just at those checkpoints.

## Target Group

Early talent participants include interns, co-ops and full-time new grads within 2 years of their graduation date.

## What It Does

- **Voice of early talent:** bi-weekly anonymous pulse check-ins capture highlights, improvement areas and goals.
- **Anonymous disengagement flags:** at-risk signals and support requests are routed to the participant's department without revealing identity.
- **Department issue trends:** common themes are surfaced per department so leaders can act on them.
- **Support & Career Connect:** an anonymous or named channel for urgent support, internal mobility and mentorship, with a 24–48 hour follow-up.
- **Career roadmap and mentors:** search real RBC roles, see a path to full-time, and request coffee chats.
- **Buddy check-ins:** cross-functional pairing and a collaborative Connections game with a leaderboard to keep participation high.

## Impact

- **Higher retention:** disengagement is caught early, before participants leave.
- **Targeted fixes:** recurring department-level issues are identified and remediated.
- **Psychological safety:** anonymity encourages honest feedback.
- **Clear career paths:** participants see concrete steps to full-time conversion.
- **Stronger networks:** cross-team pairings build connections beyond a single desk.

## Tech Stack

TanStack Start, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, Radix UI, Vite, Vitest.

## Getting Started

```sh
npm i
npm run dev
```

Other scripts: `npm run build`, `npm run test`, `npm run lint`.

> This is a front-end prototype with sample data (`src/lib/spark-data.ts`). Department flagging and issue trends are described as the intended design, not yet implemented.
